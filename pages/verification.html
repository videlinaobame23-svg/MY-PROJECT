FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does verification take?

Most applications are reviewed within 24 hours. During peak times it may take up to 48 hours.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. All uploaded documents are encrypted and only visible to our verification team. We never share them with other users.

What if I don't have a national ID?

You can use a valid passport or driver's license. If you don't have any of these, please contact support.

Can I get verified as an individual seller?

Absolutely. Verification is available to both individuals and businesses.

Can my verification be revoked?

Yes. If you violate our selling rules or receive multiple valid complaints, your verified status may be removed.
