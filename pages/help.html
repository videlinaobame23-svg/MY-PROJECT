FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from our community.

🛒 Buying

How do I buy something on IsokoRW?

Browse the category or search bar to find the item. Contact the seller via message or phone, agree on the price, and meet in a safe public place to complete the transaction. Never send money in advance.

Can I negotiate the price?

Yes. Many sellers mark items as "negotiable" and are open to offers. Use the message feature to politely negotiate before meeting.

What if the item is not as described?

Always inspect the item carefully before paying. If you notice a problem after purchase, contact the seller first. If unresolved, report the issue to our support team.

How do I save an item for later?

Click the ❤️ heart icon on any product card. It will be added to your Favorites so you can easily find it again from your account.

💼 Selling

How do I post an ad?

Click "Post an Ad" in the header, choose a category, add clear photos and a detailed description, set your price and location, then publish. Your ad goes live within minutes.

Is posting an ad free?

Yes, posting standard ads is completely free on IsokoRW. You can optionally pay to "boost" your ad for extra visibility.

How can I sell faster?

Use clear photos taken in daylight, write a detailed title and description, set a fair price, and consider boosting your ad. Verified sellers also get up to 4× more views.

How many ads can I post?

You can post unlimited ads as a verified seller. New accounts may be limited to a few active ads until they are verified.

🛡️ Safety & Trust

How do I know a seller is trustworthy?

Look for the green "✓ Verified" badge. Verified sellers have completed ID and phone verification with our team. Check their ratings and reviews from other buyers too.

What should I do to stay safe?

Always meet in a public place, bring a friend if possible, inspect the item before paying, never send money in advance, and trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, walk away.

How do I report a scam or suspicious seller?

Click the "Report" button on the listing or profile page, or contact our support team directly via the contact form below. We investigate every report within 24 hours.

💳 Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

It depends on the seller. Most accept cash on delivery, MoMo (MTN/Airtel), or bank transfer. Always confirm with the seller before meeting.

Does IsokoRW handle payments?

Not yet. Secure online payments are coming soon. For now, transactions happen directly between buyer and seller.

👤 My Account

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email. You'll receive a link to create a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to My Account → Security → Delete account. Note that this action is permanent and will remove all your listings, messages, and data.

How do I become a verified seller?

Go to the Verification page from your account or the footer, fill in the form, upload your ID and a selfie. Our team reviews within 24 hours.
